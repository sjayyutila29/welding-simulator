Safety first

Pull the helmet down (H) before striking an arc. Arc flash without a helmet is logged to the Mother Server as a safety violation.

Your job

Pick a coupon and position → choose electrode + current (E6010 / E6011 / E7018) → weld the root, hot, fill and cap passes in order. After each pass: chip, brush, then run the virtual inspection (ISO 6520-1 / 5817, AWS D1.1 / B1.11, ASME V).

📱 How to play on your phone

  • Look — two fingers · zoom — pinch
  • 📷 — camera AR on/off · tap Camera to retry if the picture is black
